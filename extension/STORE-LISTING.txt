TITLE
SOR7ED Links — Save to WhatsApp

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Meet Sorted Links: keep articles, videos and useful pages in WhatsApp. See it. Send it. SOR7ED.

FULL DESCRIPTION
That article you meant to read. That video worth another look. That useful page you know you'll need later.

Give it a place you'll remember to check: your WhatsApp thread.

SOR7ED Links — pronounced “Sorted Links” — keeps useful things close while you browse. Keep the link now. Come back when you're ready.

SEE IT. SEND IT. SOR7ED.

• Find an article, video or useful web page.
• Click the scribble, or right-click a page or link and choose “Keep with SOR7ED Links”.
• Sign in and connect WhatsApp when prompted.
• Receive the link in your WhatsApp conversation.

Your Sorted account also keeps a backup history, so you can revisit or remove saved links later.

Made for the useful things that otherwise disappear into open tabs, screenshots and “I'll find it later”.

A Sorted account and WhatsApp connection are required. You may occasionally need to reconnect WhatsApp before sending. Link previews and images depend on the original website and WhatsApp, so they are not always available.

The extension captures the page or link you choose when you click it. It does not continuously monitor your browsing history.

Learn more: https://www.sor7ed.com
